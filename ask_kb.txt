## Common questions
## Who can get Access to Work?
GOV.UK says you need a disability or health condition (physical or mental) that means you need support to do your job or get to and from work, be 16 or over, be in paid work or about to start or return to it, and live and work in England, Scotland or Wales. You don't need a diagnosis. Check the full rules on GOV.UK.
## What can Access to Work pay for?
Practical support such as equipment and software, a support worker, help with travel if you can't use public transport, and changes to the workplace. It doesn't pay for reasonable adjustments your employer should make, it doesn't affect your other benefits, and you don't pay it back.
## I'm self-employed. Can you help?
Yes. Choose "self-employed" at the timing step. GOV.UK says self-employed people need a turnover of at least £6,500 a year.
## What if I'm turned down?
GOV.UK says you can call the helpline and ask for the decision to be reconsidered. The Complete Pack includes notes and a short script for that call.
## Can you help with a problem with my employer?
No. We only help you describe your support needs for an Access to Work application. We can't advise on workplace disputes.
## Will you tell me what I'll get?
No. We help you describe your situation clearly and truthfully. Access to Work decides.
## Is a person involved?
No. Your answers are drafted by our system from what you tell us. You review and approve everything.

No. FormSorted is an independent service, not affiliated with DWP, Access to Work or GOV.UK.
